# Information Research Notes

This directory contains pre-theoretical research notes on information and related processes.

## Current focus

The current working thread investigates whether a continuous flow (`~ing`) is handled through locally usable establishments (`~ed`), and how PULL, retrospective access, relation extraction, and Operator updates may interact.

Current provisional working definition:

> **~ed = a local establishment that has become usable by the Operator for subsequent processing at that point.**

Important qualification:

- `~ed` does not imply that the result is permanently retained.
- `~ed` does not imply that it will actually be used later.
- `~ed` does not imply that later retrospective access is possible.
- A later retrospective process may create a new `~ed` from relations among earlier local establishments.

## Current comparison cases

- Automatic door
- Human recognition of an approaching ball
- Logs and retrospective inspection
- Human recall / retrospective reconstruction

## Important unresolved questions

1. What establishes the boundary of `~ed`?
2. Can something be locally established without becoming usable for later processing?
3. Is retrospective access a re-use of an earlier `~ed`, reconstruction from retained traces, or a new local establishment?
4. Are continuous retrospective comparison and on-demand retrospective PULL the same mechanism at different densities, or genuinely different mechanisms?
5. What distinguishes "OPEN_0, OPEN_1, OPEN_2" from the later establishment "it remained OPEN"?
6. When does Operator orientation matter, and when can a local establishment precede an explicit orientation?
7. Where, if anywhere, should the term "information" be placed in this process?

## Caution

This directory is intentionally pre-theoretical. Terms here must not be mapped directly to established Gyro concepts such as Slice, Stability, Trajectory, or Operator Orientation without a separate integration review.

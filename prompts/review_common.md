# Multi-AI Review Prompt

This repository stores brainstorming notes and pre-theoretical research artifacts for the Gyro Project.

The document you are reviewing is **not a finalized theory**. It is a working research note intended to expose weak assumptions, contradictions, missing cases, and alternative interpretations before formalization.

Please review the target document critically and constructively.

## Review focus

1. **Logical inconsistencies**
   - Identify contradictions, circular reasoning, hidden assumptions, or places where conclusions do not follow from the premises.

2. **Ambiguous or overloaded definitions**
   - Point out terms that are undefined, used inconsistently, or carrying multiple meanings.
   - Distinguish between wording problems and structural problems.

3. **Overlap with or divergence from existing theories**
   - Identify relevant prior theories, models, or disciplines that resemble the current idea.
   - Explain both similarities and important differences.
   - Do not force the document into an existing theory if the fit is weak.

4. **Counterexamples and falsifiability**
   - Propose concrete cases where the current model may fail.
   - Look for examples that cannot be explained naturally without adding ad hoc assumptions.
   - If possible, identify what observation would count against the current hypothesis.

5. **Overgeneralization**
   - Flag claims that appear stronger or broader than the examples justify.
   - Separate what is currently supported from what remains speculative.

6. **Next concrete test cases**
   - Suggest small, specific examples that would help test the current model.
   - Prefer simple cases that isolate one issue at a time.

7. **What may be fixed now vs. what should remain provisional**
   - Identify concepts that are mature enough for a temporary working definition.
   - Identify concepts that should remain open because fixing them now would create premature formalization.

## Review stance

- Do not be overly charitable.
- Do not assume that a new term represents a genuinely new concept.
- Do not praise novelty unless the distinction from existing work is clear.
- Do not rewrite the theory into a more familiar framework merely to make it easier to explain.
- Preserve uncertainty where the source document is intentionally provisional.
- Distinguish carefully between:
  - explanatory examples,
  - evidence,
  - hypotheses,
  - working definitions,
  - established conclusions.

## Output format

Please structure the review as:

### 1. Overall assessment

A concise assessment of the current maturity and strongest/weakest aspects.

### 2. Strong points

Only points that materially strengthen the research direction.

### 3. Critical issues

Rank issues by importance. Explain why each matters.

### 4. Counterexamples / failure cases

Provide concrete examples intended to stress or break the current model.

### 5. Related theories or prior work

List only genuinely relevant comparisons and explain the correspondence and mismatch.

### 6. What should remain provisional

Identify terms or claims that should not yet be formalized.

### 7. Recommended next steps

Give a small number of specific next investigations, ordered by priority.

---

## Important context

The goal of this review cycle is not to produce a polished theory quickly.

The intended workflow is:

1. Brainstorm and clarify the idea.
2. Commit a coherent research note.
3. Obtain independent reviews from multiple AI systems.
4. Preserve those critiques as review history.
5. Revisit the idea and update the research note.
6. Repeat until the model either becomes robust enough to formalize or is rejected/reframed.
